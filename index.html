Cockpit

My whole day in one window.

macOSSwiftUIIn daily use View on GitHub
  • A day dial. One ring shows what’s done, what’s planned and what needs me next.
  • Signals, not inboxes. Mail and messages are sorted down to the few things that need a reply.
  • One timeline. macOS Calendar and Notion tasks together, with clashes flagged.
  • A window that follows the sky. Dawn, day, dusk and night looks change with the time.

Built with SwiftUI, EventKit, the Notion API, Apple Music and Gemini.

Cairn

Every market, one portfolio.

iPhoneSwiftUIPrivate · on request
  • Why it exists. Most trackers do Europe or the US. Cairn holds both, plus Tokyo and crypto, all in euro.
  • Five lenses. Benchmark twin, currency X-ray, true diversification, dividend radar and smart invest.
  • A daily brief. Gemini explains what moved and answers questions about the portfolio.
  • Silver finish. Colours sampled from the silver iPhone 17 Pro it runs on.

Built with SwiftUI, Swift 6, WidgetKit, Live Activities and the Gemini API. Screens show the demo portfolio.

AeroCommand

Run an airline from your iPad.

iPadMetal · RealityKitPrivate project
  • A real world. Hundreds of airports laid out from OpenStreetMap, with terrain, in 3D.
  • Deep, not busy. Routes, fare buckets, slots and rivals, then cargo, rail, business jets and air taxis.
  • Follow any flight. From pushback to landing, with a photo mode.
  • Two looks. Night-ops satellite or a light chart-paper map.

Built with SwiftUI, Metal and RealityKit. Screens show the built-in demo airline.

Waybill

A logistics idle game in toy-scale 3D.

iPadRealityKitVersion 1.0 View on GitHub
  • Five regions. Riverside, Harbour City, Granite Pass, Metro and finally Orbit.
  • Trucks that really drive. They follow real turning circles, reverse into bays and never overlap.
  • All made in code. Every model, plus my own edge smoothing and shading.
  • Real time. Day and night follow the clock; the depot keeps working while you’re away.

Built with RealityKit, SwiftUI and Metal, balanced with a Python simulation.

Home Hub

A calm screen for the living room.

iPad Air 2 · iOS 15SwiftUIPrivate project
  • The house at a glance. Sonos, the TV, the PlayStation, solar panels and the dryer.
  • Out and about. Rain radar, trains, bin days and the family calendar.
  • Calm by default. Alerts come and go as pop-ups. Goals get the whole screen.
  • On old hardware. Runs on a 2014 iPad at about 2% CPU and 20 MB.

Built with SwiftUI for iOS 15 and the local APIs of the devices in the house.
